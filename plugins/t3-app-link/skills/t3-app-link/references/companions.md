# Optional Slopware companions

Adapted from codex-app-link's companions reference at `3372f52`.

T3 App Link installs alone. Apply companions already required by the project or
invoked by the user:

- MSW admits only work whose deletion leaves the requested outcome unmet or
  unproven. Verify necessary claims and stop when the outcome is proven.
- MSL keeps reports focused on the facts the reader must act on or check.
- Timebox applies an authorized AWT/CGP window. Preserve its original clock on
  resumes; CGP is closeout time rather than another work window.

Read the installed companion's instructions before applying it. Do not assume a
worker has the manager's skill catalog. Put the necessary outcome and constraints
in its handoff. Missing companions do not expand its assignment.

Do not invent work durations or counts. When a timebox is required but its
necessary durations are unresolved, ask the owner. Otherwise use the current
assignment's authority and proof criteria.

Install companions only when the user requests it. A newly installed skill may
require a new provider session before it appears in that session's catalog.
